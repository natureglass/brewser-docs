## 1. Sign in

Submissions require a Google account. On the Switch itself, Brewser uses the
device-code sign-in flow — you'll get a short code to enter on your phone or
computer.

## 2. Fill in the submission form

On [brewser.io](https://brewser.io), open the publisher dashboard and
create a new submission:

- **App name and ID** — IDs follow the `com_yourname_appname` convention
- **Description and category** — remember, tools and experiments are
  first-class citizens alongside games
- **Platform labels** — which devices/targets your app supports
- **Icon** and screenshots

## 4. Track your submission

The pipeline is GitHub-backed: after submission you can watch build/review
status directly from the dashboard, which mirrors the live GitHub check
status.

Once approved and security-cleared, the app appears in the catalogue.
Downloads and ratings start flowing into your publisher stats automatically.

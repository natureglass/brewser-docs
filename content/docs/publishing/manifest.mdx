Every Brewser app ships a manifest describing its identity and the hardware
capabilities it needs.

## Why declare permissions?

Users see the required capabilities **before** launching your app, and the
runtime only exposes the hardware APIs you declared. Combined with the
runtime device picker, this means no app ever silently accesses hardware.

## Declarable capabilities

- `serial` — WebSerial (USB CDC devices such as ESP32/Arduino)
- `bluetooth` — WebBluetooth
- `midi` — Web MIDI
- `nfc` — Web NFC
- `usb` — WebUSB

## Best practice

Declare the minimum you need. Apps requesting no hardware access at all skip
the permission prompt entirely and offer the smoothest click-to-play
experience.

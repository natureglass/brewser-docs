The catalogue at [play.brewser.io](https://play.brewser.io) is where all
published apps live.

## Click-to-play

Apps load in an engagement-friendly model: you see the app's card, click to
start, and the runtime takes over — with proper focus management so gamepad
input goes where you expect.

## Ratings & downloads

Every app shows community ratings and download counts, aggregated from
anonymous telemetry.

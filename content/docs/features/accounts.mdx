Brewser accounts are Google-backed — no separate password to manage.

## Signing in on the Switch

The Switch has no comfortable way to type an email and password, so Brewser
uses the **device-code flow**: the console shows a short code, you visit a
Google URL on your phone or computer, enter the code, and you're signed in.
Verified end-to-end on real hardware.

## What an account gets you

- Achievements and progress tracking
- Rating apps
- Participating in the Ideas Board
- Publishing apps (publisher dashboard)

## Privacy

Authentication is handled by Google; Brewser stores only a salted hash of
your Google subject ID — never your password, and not your email address in
plain form. See the Privacy Policy on brewser.tech for details.

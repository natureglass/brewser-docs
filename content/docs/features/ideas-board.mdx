Got an idea for an app but not the time to build it? Post it on the Ideas
Board on [brewser.io](https://brewser.io).

## How it works

- **Post an idea** — describe the app you wish existed
- **Upvote** ideas you'd use
- **Mark interest in building** — developers can signal they're picking an
  idea up
- **Built!** — when an idea ships, it links directly to the published app,
  and discussion opens up in the comments

It's a lightweight way to match tinkerers who want things with tinkerers who
build things.

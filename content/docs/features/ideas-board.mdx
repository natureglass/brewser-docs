If you have an idea for an app but not the time to build it, post it on the
Ideas Board on [brewser.io](https://brewser.io).

## How it works

- **Post an idea**: describe the app you wish existed
- **Upvote** ideas you'd use
- **Mark interest in building**: developers can signal they're picking an
  idea up
- **Built**: when an idea ships, it links directly to the published app, and
  discussion opens in the comments

It connects people who want apps with the developers who build them.

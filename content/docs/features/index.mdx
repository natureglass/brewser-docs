---
title: Features
description: What the Brewser platform offers
---

Brewser is more than a runtime. It also includes a small ecosystem of features:

- **[The catalogue](/docs/features/catalogue)**: browse and launch apps with
  click-to-play
- **[Accounts](/docs/features/accounts)**: sign in with Google, even from the
  Switch itself
- **[Achievements](/docs/features/achievements)**: earn badges as you explore
- **[Saves & Leaderboards](/docs/features/saves-and-leaderboards)**: cloud
  saves that follow you across devices, and shared scoreboards
- **[Ideas Board](/docs/features/ideas-board)**: request apps, upvote ideas,
  and see them get built

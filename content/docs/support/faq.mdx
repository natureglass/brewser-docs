## Is Brewser free?

Yes. Brewser is free to use, and [publishing](/docs/publishing) is free too. There's no paid tier.

## Is this piracy? Is it legal?

No, and it's designed not to be. Brewser ships **no** Nintendo code, firmware, ROMs, games, or
encryption keys, and it can't run them — it runs your own web apps (HTML/JS/CSS/WebGL/WASM). It's an
independent homebrew project, **not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo**. You're responsible
for how you use it and for complying with the terms of any service your apps connect to. See
[Legal & Attributions](/docs/support/legal).

## Do I need custom firmware? Does it work on a stock Switch?

You need custom firmware — Brewser runs as homebrew under
[Atmosphère](https://github.com/Atmosphere-NX/Atmosphere). It does **not** work on a stock,
un-modified Switch. If you're starting from zero, the [NH Switch Guide](https://switch.hacks.guide/)
is the place to begin. See [Installing](/docs/getting-started/installing).

## Which consoles are supported?

Any Nintendo Switch that can run Atmosphère — the original (Erista), the revised model (Mariko),
the Switch Lite, and the OLED model. The runtime targets the Tegra X1 these all share.

## Can I get banned for using this online?

Running **any** homebrew online on a custom-firmware console carries a real, non-zero risk to your
Nintendo account and console — this is true of the whole CFW ecosystem, not something specific to
Brewser. Brewser needs a network connection to load the [catalogue](/docs/features/catalogue),
download apps, and use [accounts, saves and leaderboards](/docs/features/accounts).

If you want to minimise that risk, the community-standard advice applies:

- Use an **emuMMC / emuNAND** setup rather than your sysNAND, so your "clean" system stays separate.
- Consider blocking Nintendo's servers (e.g. **90DNS**) on the setup you use for homebrew.
- Understand that no approach is a guarantee. You accept the risk.

**Prefer to avoid the question entirely?** Most Brewser apps are fully self-contained and make no
network calls at all — you can browse the catalogue over Wi-Fi, download what you want, and then run
those apps offline. Only the catalogue, sign-in, and cloud features need connectivity.

## Do I have to sign in?

No. Sign-in (with Google) is only needed for cloud [saves and
leaderboards](/docs/features/saves-and-leaderboards), achievements, and
[publishing](/docs/publishing). Everything else — browsing and running apps — works signed out. See
[Privacy & Your Data](/docs/support/privacy) for what's stored.

## Where do I report bugs or get help?

See [Getting Help](/docs/support). A minimal repro plus "here's what Chrome does" is the most useful
kind of report.

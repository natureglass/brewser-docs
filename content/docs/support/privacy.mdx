This page explains what Brewser stores when you use accounts and cloud features. Brewser is
operated from the EU and aims to keep to the spirit of the GDPR: collect little, be clear about it,
and let you delete it.

If you never sign in, Brewser stores no personal account data about you — you can browse and run
apps signed out. The rest of this page is about what happens once you do sign in.

## Sign-in

Accounts are **Google-backed** — you sign in with Google, and Brewser never sees or stores a
password. Sign-in is verified against Google directly (OpenID Connect); the only third-party network
involved in authentication is Google's own endpoints.

To recognise you across sessions and devices, Brewser stores your **Google account identifier** (the
"subject" id Google issues). This links your saves, leaderboard entries and achievements to you. If
you publish apps, your developer profile also stores your email and display name. See
[Accounts & Sign-in](/docs/features/accounts) for the sign-in side.

## What's stored, by feature

| Feature | What's stored |
| --- | --- |
| **Cloud saves** | Your save data for each app, linked to your account. Only you can read your own saves; an app only ever sees its own save box. |
| **Leaderboards** | The score and the display name you submit, linked to your account. Leaderboards are public — other players see your name and score. |
| **Achievements** | Which badges you've earned. |
| **Favourites & ratings** | Which apps you've favourited or rated. |
| **Publisher profile** (if you publish) | The profile you create: display name, username, email, and anything you add — website, bio, donation link, avatar — plus your submission history. |
| **Donations** | Only a link you choose to show. **No payment details ever touch Brewser** — donating happens entirely on the service you link to. |

Anonymous, non-personal counts (an app's total downloads and ratings) are also kept, but they
aren't tied to your identity.

## Deleting your data

Several things you can remove yourself, today:

- **A cloud save** — an app can clear its own cloud save for you.
- **A leaderboard entry** — you can remove your own entry from a board.
- **Ideas / comments** you posted on the [Ideas Board](/docs/features/ideas-board).
- **A published app** — you can unpublish or delete your submissions from the dashboard.

To delete your whole account and everything associated with it, contact the Brewser team through
[brewser.tech](https://brewser.tech); we action data-deletion requests within 30 days. Your data is
kept only while your account is active — nothing here is sold or shared with third parties.

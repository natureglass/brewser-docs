Brewser is an evolving runtime. This page collects the bigger things being worked toward. These are
intentions, not promises. For what doesn't work yet, see
[Known Limitations](/docs/support/limitations); for shipped changes, see the
[Changelog](/docs/support/changelog).

## On the radar

- **Wider WebGL 2 conformance.** Common paths are solid (major Three.js scenes run on real
  hardware), and the long tail of conformance is being closed. Reports of Chrome-vs-Brewser
  differences directly feed this work.
- **Unity WebGL 2 exports.** WebGL 1 Unity exports work today; WebGL 2 export support is in
  progress. See [Unity WebGL Exports](/docs/tips/unity).
- **Hardware-accelerated video decode.** Video currently decodes in software, which limits
  high-resolution and next-gen codecs; engaging the console's hardware video block is a goal. See
  [Audio & Media](/docs/runtime/audio-and-media).
- **On-console account bridge for the SDK.** Making authenticated cloud sync automatic for every app
  on the console (so apps don't each need to wire up the token) is designed and in progress. See the
  [`brewser.js` SDK](/docs/features/brewser-sdk).

## Influencing the roadmap

The [Ideas Board](/docs/features/ideas-board) is the most direct way to influence what gets built:
post an app or feature idea, or upvote existing ones.

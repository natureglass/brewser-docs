## Gamepad

Joy-Cons and Pro Controllers surface through the standard **Gamepad API**
(`navigator.getGamepads()`). Buttons and axes follow the standard mapping, so
gamepad-aware web games generally work unmodified.

## Touch

The Switch touchscreen fires standard **Touch events** (and pointer events).
Remember that touch only works in handheld mode.

## Motion / orientation

**DeviceOrientationEvent** is implemented to W3C spec, mapped to the Switch's
landscape physical frame and verified against real hardware — tilting the
console upright reports β=+90°, rolling right reports γ=−90°, exactly as a
phone in landscape would.
